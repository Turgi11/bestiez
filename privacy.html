English summary

Bestiez ("בסטיז") is a virtual pixel-art pet app for iPhone. There are no accounts, no logins, no chat, no user-generated content, no ads, no tracking and no analytics SDK.

Everything the app knows about you — the character you picked, the name you gave it, its gender, mood, streak, onboarding state and theme — is stored only on your device and in the app group shared with the widgets. It is never sent anywhere and we cannot see it.

Exactly three parties receive data. RevenueCat (USA) handles subscriptions and receives purchase history, country and device info under an anonymous app user id that is not your name, phone or email; its dashboard shows us account-level subscription totals built from that data, never the behaviour of an identified person. Expo's update service is contacted on launch to check for a JavaScript update, receiving platform, runtime version and an installation id. Apple processes the purchase and the receipt; we never see payment card details. None of this is used for advertising.

Notifications are local only; there is no push token and no server of ours. The app requests no camera, photo library, microphone, location or motion access: those permissions are not declared in the binary at all.

Deleting the app deletes the on-device data.
